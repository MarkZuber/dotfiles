# bro

Restate your own last message in plain language. Do no new work. Run no tools. Look
nothing up. Everything you need is already on the screen.

Say the same thing, but:

- **Plain human language.** Drop the jargon. If a term has no everyday equivalent,
  keep the term and say what it means in one short clause.
- **Shorter.** Cut the hedging. Cut the throat-clearing. Cut anything the reader can
  already see for themselves.
- **Like one person talking to another.** Not a report. Not a wall of bullets.
- **Same facts.** Paths, commands, numbers and names stay exactly as they were.
  Simpler wording must not mean a simpler truth. If something was uncertain, it is
  still uncertain — say so in plain words rather than dropping the caveat.

If the last message was already plain, say that in one sentence. Do not pad out a
rewrite for the sake of answering.
